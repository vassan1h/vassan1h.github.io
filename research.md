My research sits at the interface of computational biology and translational medicine, with a strong emphasis on rigorous, reproducible analysis.

## Domains
- **Single-cell and spatial genomics:** cell-state mapping, lineage/trajectory analysis, and tissue-level interpretation.
- **Clinical bioinformatics:** analysis workflows for gastrointestinal and epithelial disease cohorts.
- **Metagenomics and systems biology:** community profiling, de novo assembly pipelines, and network-level interpretation.

## Methods and tooling
- **Pipeline engineering:** Nextflow, Snakemake, Docker, HPC environments.
- **Analysis stack:** R, Python, statistical modeling, and machine-learning workflows.
- **Multi-omics modalities:** scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, WES, WGS, and related integration workflows.

## Collaboration interests
I am especially interested in projects that connect high-dimensional omics data to actionable biological hypotheses, biomarker development, and clinically relevant translational outcomes.
